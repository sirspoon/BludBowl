# What's new

The highlights, newest first. Every change in full: [the full patch log](https://github.com/sirspoon/BludBowl/blob/main/CHANGELOG-FULL.md).

## v5.07 (16 Nov 2026)
- HD sprite test (`?hd`): the High Elves redrawn after Games Workshop's new High Elf team, so each position stands out at a
  glance: silver Linemen with a team-colour crest, White Lions under a tawny lion pelt with a clawed arm raised, Phoenix
  Warriors in a tall gold winged helm and gold scale coat, Dragon Princes in blue.

## v5.06 (16 Nov 2026)
- The computer's throwers can pick up a loose ball and pass it on in one go to an open team-mate, as human coaches often do.

## v5.05 (16 Nov 2026)
- A test of sharper graphics: High Elves drawn with twice the pixel detail. Add `?hd` to the address, or turn on
  Settings > HD sprites (test), to see them; nothing changes otherwise.

## v5.04 (16 Nov 2026)
- The computer plans risky plays (a Blitz needing a Rush or dodge, a hard pick-up, a run in to score) counting on a team
  re-roll when it has one to spare, instead of only re-rolling after a failure.

## v5.03 (16 Nov 2026)
- The computer throws a pass on the last turn of a half when you don't get another turn (it costs nothing then, and a completion
  earns its passer Star Player Points), as strong human coaches often do.

## v5.02 (15 Nov 2026)
- The computer now uses a team re-roll on a failed Bone Head, Really Stupid or Animal Savagery roll when that player's action
  matters (a Blitz, a hand-off or pass, or carrying the ball) and it can spare one.

## v5.01 (15 Nov 2026)
- Da Red Gobbo's Squigmobile no longer looks cut off on its right-hand side: the elf's sock, the bomb's fuse and the
  candy-cane runner now stop short of the edge with an outline.

## v5.00 (14 Nov 2026)
- **New Star Player: Da Red Gobbo's Squigmobile** (200k, Exhibition Play only, any team but Dwarfs): a Goblin war machine
  with Bombardier, Hail Mary Pass (bombs thrown anywhere on the pitch), Juggernaut, Stand Firm, Mighty Blow and Secret Weapon,
  and **Have a Present, Ya Git**: an extra Bribe each half, lost if it isn't used. Drawn after the festive miniature.

Everything else since 4.0, in brief:
- **Training mode** and **Help**: play the computer with a coach who explains every roll, block, pass and turnover as it
  happens, a step-by-step start of the match, a tour of the screen, and Help topics with pictures.
- **Rulesets** (was Tournament Rules): GW Matched Play, Glory on the Gridiron 2 and UKTC XI built in, or your own; tournaments
  and teams built to them are checked, and computer teams are drafted to them. GW's league and Sevens rules shown too.
- **Team Builder** on tabs in an always-visible summary box, styled drop-down menus everywhere, a filter on the Teams screen,
  and a printable **Roster PDF** with keywords, skill descriptions and a ruleset check.
- **Chaos Wastes**: a new pitch with its own music, and the Chaos Wastes weather table (exhibitions, two-device matches and
  the Glory on the Gridiron 2 tournament).
- **My matches**: every match is recorded; save one to watch again in I Like to Watch, with the coaches' decisions and the
  close-up replays (now also for kick-offs and Kick Team-mate).
- **New sprites** for 30 Star Players (Varag after the owner's painted miniature), and every player breathes while standing.
- **A tougher computer**: it blocks more and hurts more, orders its blocks, Blitzes like strong coaches, screens its ball
  carrier and keeps defenders back sensibly. (And it can now play inside BotBowl, for testing.)
- Secret options: **Fancy Socks** Morg and **Your Mum**.
- **Fixes**: full screen on Chrome, big text sizes, touch-screen skill tags, saved league matches at full time, and more.

## v4.00 (29 Oct 2026)
Everything since 3.0, in brief:
- **I Like to Watch** (main menu): watch any match from FUMBBL on BludBowl's own pitch. Type its number or pick from the featured matches (FUMBBL's recent top games, or our picks). Smooth moves, flying balls and thrown team-mates, the game's own roll, block, re-roll and choice windows showing each coach's decision, kick-off events, touchdowns and turnovers, a running commentary with the dice, a camera that follows the action, a turn picker, speeds from ½× to 8×, and tap a player for their card. With match music, and the screen stays on.
- **A much stronger computer**, taught by 300 matches of strong FUMBBL coaches: it sets up, receives, cages and orders each turn like them, saves its re-rolls for what matters, blocks more, goes all out for the end zone late in a half, and throws team-mates and bombs.
- **Online** (main menu): Play a friend on another device, with voice chat, recent opponents, reconnecting and the same match settings as an exhibition; shared leagues; and online tournaments.
- **Saved matches:** a league or tournament match each keeps its own save, alongside an exhibition, so you can leave one half-played and come back to it.
- **Formations** are saved per race, with one set for defence and one for offence.
- **14 new pitches**, a home ground for nearly every team, each with its own scenery and music: Brimstone, Woodland, Warped Flagstones, Black Ark, Orc Shantytown, Dwarf Hold, Ogre Wastes, Plague Garden, Bretonnian Castle, Ziggurat, Altdorf, Frozen Fjord, Mootland Green and Khemri. The crowd taunts the ref, and a pixel-art referee jumps out for every sending-off.
- **New player figures** in a more detailed style for most teams.
- **Easier to play:** bigger action buttons, moves in two taps with the route shown first, keyboard shortcuts (and WASD to look around), and saved teams numbered as in the Team Builder.
- **Fixes:** freezes, two-device play, the inducements panel, league pitches, replays and more.

## v3.00 (15 Oct 2026)
- All 30 teams from the 2025 rules and every Star Player; Play a friend on another device; shared leagues; blocks played out on the pitch with slow-motion replays of passes, throws and bombs; weather on the pitch; and much more. See the full patch log.
