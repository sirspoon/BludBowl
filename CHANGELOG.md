# What's new

The highlights, newest first. Every change in full: [the full patch log](https://github.com/sirspoon/BludBowl/blob/main/CHANGELOG-FULL.md).

## v5.84 (28 Nov 2026)
- The Experimental computer coach (brain x) makes use of a Blitzer's movement after his block (picking up a loose ball, carrying the ball on, or moving where he's needed), passes from closer in (a Rush allowed), and no longer tries pick-ups below 45%.

## v5.83 (27 Nov 2026)
- The Experimental computer coach (brain x) gets its players off the sideline: one pinned there by an opponent dodges clear at the end of its blocks, and a block's follow-up is taken when it steps off the edge.

## v5.82 (27 Nov 2026)
- The Experimental computer coach (brain x) pushes players into the crowd on purpose: it fills the squares a player on the edge could be pushed into, then blocks or Blitzes him off the pitch, and pins players against the sideline for next turn.

## v5.81 (27 Nov 2026)
- Humans have a built-in "Default (classic)" team in the team menu: BotBowl's competition line-up (2 Blitzers, a Thrower, 2 Catchers, 7 Linemen, no Ogre or Halfling, 3 re-rolls), the team drefsante plays best against.

## v5.80 (27 Nov 2026)
- Playing drefsante: it's told each player's agility on its own rules' scale, so it no longer thinks an Ogre is more agile than a Catcher.

## v5.79 (27 Nov 2026)
- Playing drefsante (the local-server computer coach): it now carries on with a player when its plan says so (moving on after a Blitz, naming the catcher after running to throw), and its ball carrier no longer gets walked on by the Experimental coach after a pick-up.

## v5.78 (26 Nov 2026)
- The experimental computer coach (brain x) has a new kick-off trick: when receiving, it gangs up on a player standing out by the sideline, with one player stepping in to help and another Blitzing him.

## v5.77 (25 Nov 2026)
- At full time a window asks whether to save the match, with a name for it you can change.
- The local-server computer coach no longer pauses twice before each push, follow-up and move.

## v5.76 (25 Nov 2026)
- Your last three matches are kept automatically in I Like to Watch > My matches (a fourth replaces the oldest). Press Keep on one to hold on to it, and Rename to give it your own name. Switch it off in Settings > Keep my last matches.

## v5.75 (25 Nov 2026)
- The computer coach from a local AI server only shows in Settings when that server is running, and a phone on the same Wi-Fi can play against it too.
- During a match against it, a small light in the corner shows it is really playing (green, pulsing) or that another coach is standing in (amber, with the reason).

## v5.74 (25 Nov 2026)
- A new kind of computer coach: an AI provided by a server running on your own computer. When one is running, Settings > Computer coach offers it. Local play only.

## v5.73 (25 Nov 2026)
- The experimental computer coach (brain x) plays the end of each half better: the turn before the last it sends receivers up the pitch, and on the last turn one runs into the end zone for a throw.

## v5.72 (23 Nov 2026)
- The computer's blocks are quicker: its block dice land faster and it picks its die sooner.
- Armour and injury dice no longer pop up at the top by default (they could lag behind the pitch); switch them back on in Settings > Armour and injury dice.

## v5.71 (23 Nov 2026)
- New dice sounds, built from a real throw: block dice rattle quietly while they wait for your tap, then each die clacks down on the exact moment it lands and bounces, with a little tumbling patter after. Other rolls get the same sound.

## v5.70 (23 Nov 2026)

## v5.69 (23 Nov 2026)

## v4.00 (29 Oct 2026)
Everything since 3.0, in brief:
- **I Like to Watch** (main menu): watch any match from FUMBBL on BludBowl's own pitch. Type its number or pick from the featured matches (FUMBBL's recent top games, or our picks). Smooth moves, flying balls and thrown team-mates, the game's own roll, block, re-roll and choice windows showing each coach's decision, kick-off events, touchdowns and turnovers, a running commentary with the dice, a camera that follows the action, a turn picker, speeds from ½× to 8×, and tap a player for their card. With match music, and the screen stays on.
- **A much stronger computer**, taught by 300 matches of strong FUMBBL coaches: it sets up, receives, cages and orders each turn like them, saves its re-rolls for what matters, blocks more, goes all out for the end zone late in a half, and throws team-mates and bombs.
- **Online** (main menu): Play a friend on another device, with voice chat, recent opponents, reconnecting and the same match settings as an exhibition; shared leagues; and online tournaments.
- **Saved matches:** a league or tournament match each keeps its own save, alongside an exhibition, so you can leave one half-played and come back to it.
- **Formations** are saved per race, with one set for defence and one for offence.
- **14 new pitches**, a home ground for nearly every team, each with its own scenery and music: Brimstone, Woodland, Warped Flagstones, Black Ark, Orc Shantytown, Dwarf Hold, Ogre Wastes, Plague Garden, Bretonnian Castle, Ziggurat, Altdorf, Frozen Fjord, Mootland Green and Khemri. The crowd taunts the ref, and a pixel-art referee jumps out for every sending-off.
- **New player figures** in a more detailed style for most teams.
- **Easier to play:** bigger action buttons, moves in two taps with the route shown first, keyboard shortcuts (and WASD to look around), and saved teams numbered as in the Team Builder.
- **Fixes:** freezes, two-device play, the inducements panel, league pitches, replays and more.
