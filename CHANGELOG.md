## v4.00 (29 Oct 2026)
Everything since 3.0, in brief:
- **I Like to Watch** (main menu): watch any match from FUMBBL on BludBowl's own pitch. Type its number or pick from the featured matches (FUMBBL's recent top games, or our picks). Smooth moves, flying balls and thrown team-mates, the game's own roll, block, re-roll and choice windows showing each coach's decision, kick-off events, touchdowns and turnovers, a running commentary with the dice, a camera that follows the action, a turn picker, speeds from ½× to 8×, and tap a player for their card. With match music, and the screen stays on.
- **A much stronger computer**, taught by 300 matches of strong FUMBBL coaches: it sets up, receives, cages and orders each turn like them, saves its re-rolls for what matters, blocks more, goes all out for the end zone late in a half, and throws team-mates and bombs.
- **Online** (main menu): Play a friend on another device, with voice chat, recent opponents, reconnecting and the same match settings as an exhibition; shared leagues; and online tournaments.
- **Saved matches:** a league or tournament match each keeps its own save, alongside an exhibition, so you can leave one half-played and come back to it.
- **Formations** are saved per race, with one set for defence and one for offence.
- **14 new pitches**, a home ground for nearly every team, each with its own scenery and music: Brimstone, Woodland, Warped Flagstones, Black Ark, Orc Shantytown, Dwarf Hold, Ogre Wastes, Plague Garden, Bretonnian Castle, Ziggurat, Altdorf, Frozen Fjord, Mootland Green and Khemri. The crowd taunts the ref, and a pixel-art referee jumps out for every sending-off.
- **New player figures** in a more detailed style for most teams.
- **Easier to play:** bigger action buttons, moves in two taps with the route shown first, keyboard shortcuts (and WASD to look around), and saved teams numbered as in the Team Builder.
- **Fixes:** freezes, two-device play, the inducements panel, league pitches, replays and more.
